# Wheel of Life

An interactive self-assessment tool to help you visualize and track satisfaction across 11 essential life domains.

## What It Does

The Wheel of Life provides a visual snapshot of your current life balance. Rate each category from 1-5 to see where you're thriving and where you need attention. Add reflective notes to turn insights into actionable goals.

## How to Use

1. Open `wheel-of-life.html` in any web browser
2. Click on a category (either on the wheel or in the list)
3. Rate it from 1-5 (1 = lowest, 5 = highest)
4. Add optional notes about what's working or needs improvement
5. Repeat for all categories

Your progress is automatically saved in your browser.

## Categories

- **Mission** - Purpose and calling
- **Family** - Family relationships
- **Friends** - Social connections
- **Romance** - Romantic partnership
- **Spiritual** - Spiritual life and beliefs
- **Mental** - Mental and emotional wellbeing
- **Physical** - Physical health and vitality
- **Growth** - Personal development
- **Money** - Financial health
- **Career** - Work and professional life
- **Joy** - Fun, play, and enjoyment

## Features

- Interactive visual wheel showing your life balance
- 1-5 scoring system (with gentle discouragement of "3" scores)
- Contextual reflection prompts based on your scores
- Automatic saving to browser localStorage
- Mobile-friendly responsive design
- Reset functionality to start fresh

## Files

- `wheel-of-life.html` - Main application
- `script.js` - Application logic
- `style.css` - Styling and layout

## Tips

- Be honest with your scores - this is for you
- Avoid selecting "3" - challenge yourself to choose 2 or 4
- Review your wheel monthly or quarterly to track progress
- Use your notes to create specific goals and habits
