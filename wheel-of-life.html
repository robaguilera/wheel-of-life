💡 What is the Wheel of Life?

▼

Audit Your Life

This exercise provides a visual snapshot of your current satisfaction across the essential domains of life. It reveals where you stand today—honestly and objectively.

Identify What Needs Attention

By mapping your scores visually, you'll immediately see which areas are thriving and which need work. This clarity transforms vague dissatisfaction into specific, actionable insights.

Focus Your Energy Wisely

Life is about trade-offs and priorities. This tool helps you make conscious decisions about where to invest your time, energy, and resources. Instead of reacting to whatever screams loudest, you can strategically focus on the areas that will create the most meaningful impact in your life.

📊 Scoring Guide

▼

5 out of 5: You cannot imagine your life being any better in this particular domain

1 out of 5: You cannot imagine your life being any worse in this particular domain

Avoid selecting "3": The middle ground is too average—it's neither low enough to spark immediate action, nor high enough to say "I'm truly happy here." Challenge yourself to choose 2 or 4 to reflect your honest position. A "3" often indicates you need to dig deeper.

🎯 What's Next? Taking Action

▼

Turn Insights into Action

Your completed wheel is just the beginning. The real value comes from using these insights to create meaningful change in your life.

For Low-Scoring Areas (1-2)

Create improvement goals: Use your notes to identify 1-3 specific actions you can take this month. Start small—sustainable change beats ambitious failure. Turn your "how can I improve" notes into concrete weekly habits.

For High-Scoring Areas (4-5)

Build maintenance habits: Don't let success become complacency. Review your "what's going well" notes and schedule the activities that keep this area strong. Make them non-negotiable parts of your routine.

Review and Adjust

Revisit your wheel monthly or quarterly. Track which areas improve and which decline. This isn't a one-time exercise—it's a living tool for continuous growth and balance.
